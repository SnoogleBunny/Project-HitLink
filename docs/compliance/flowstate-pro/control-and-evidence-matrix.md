# Flowstate Pro control and evidence matrix

**Status:** SPECIFIED on 2026-09-23; G0 remains `BLOCKED`; WP1.x is unauthorized

**Scope:** Flowstate Pro only; Virginia profiles first and British Columbia private-clinic profiles through a separate gate

**Authority:** `decision-register-and-open-items.md`, `product-requirements-document.md`, `system-design.md`, `security-privacy-architecture.md`, `roadmap.md`, `legal-applicability-matrix.md`, `data-classification-and-flows.md`, and the detailed implementation plan

**Non-claim:** This matrix specifies planned controls and evidence contracts. It does not establish implementation, operating effectiveness, legal review, vendor approval, profile approval, production readiness, or compliance.

## 1. Reading and status rules

- `SPECIFIED` means only that the control, test, exercise, evidence contract, cadence, and gate are defined. It does not mean `IMPLEMENTED`, `TECHNICALLY_VERIFIED`, `OPERATIONALLY_EXERCISED`, `EVIDENCE_COMPLETE`, `LEGALLY_REVIEWED`, or `APPROVED_FOR_PROFILE`.
- `BLOCKED` identifies an unresolved dependency that prevents the affected gate or runtime activation. Any applicable blocker keeps the tenant, profile, module, destination, or release disabled or `REVIEW_BLOCKED`; resolving a blocker does not activate it automatically.
- Counsel-owned applicability and policy values remain `COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. No row may infer `NOT_APPLICABLE`; qualified reviewers must record scope, reason, source, approver, profile/version, and review date.
- Tests are repeatable technical or documentary checks. Exercises are human or operational rehearsals and cannot be replaced by tests. Evidence must bind the exact candidate, environment, profile, configuration, producer, reviewer, date, result, hash where suitable, retention, and protected location.
- Contracts, legal advice, personnel files, incident bodies, secrets, production exports, and assessor workpapers stay outside Git. Repository records contain only approved templates, redacted metadata, hashes/manifests, and protected evidence references.
- All implementation targets below are planned. No target is an implementation claim, and G0 must be approved before any WP1.x implementation begins.

## 2. Accountable functions

| Function | Accountability boundary |
|---|---|
| Executive risk owner | Resources the program; accepts authorized residual risk; approves claims, launch, and exceptions with required reviewers. |
| Product owner | Owns product/profile scope, activation intent, workflow/content requirements, commercial-trigger routing, and packet sequencing without supplying legal, security, or production approval. |
| Architecture owner | Owns approved system boundaries, dependency/resource topology, Standard/Pro and country-plane separation, and architecture change review. |
| Security Official | Owns security governance, risk analysis, access safeguards, technical-control oversight, and security evidence. |
| Privacy lead | Owns privacy governance, responsibility allocation, purpose/authority coordination, rights, lifecycle, and privacy incident coordination. |
| Qualified legal counsel | Makes customer/profile legal-applicability and legal-policy determinations; software and documentation cannot substitute. |
| Incident commander | Commands detection, containment, clocks, evidence, communications routing, recovery, and closure. |
| Communications owner | Coordinates approved customer, regulator, public, sales, support, and internal incident wording and channels; cannot decide reportability or publish without required Legal/Privacy approval. |
| Platform/Operations | Operates infrastructure, identity administration, monitoring, backup/restore, DR, deployment, access, and continuity procedures. |
| Engineering | Implements approved application, data, policy, audit, file, and integration controls and maintains focused tests. |
| Database owner | Owns Pro schema, tenant integrity, RLS, migrations, lifecycle persistence, recovery, and database evidence. |
| QA/Assurance | Independently verifies tests, exercises, evidence completeness, negative cases, and release reconciliation. |
| Independent assessor | Performs the qualified independent assessment and retest for the approved scope; implementation teams cannot substitute for or unilaterally close this function's findings. |
| Vendor owner/Procurement | Owns due diligence, agreement metadata, covered configuration, region, support, renewal, change, and termination review. |
| People owner | Owns screening, workforce acknowledgements, training administration, sanctions process, and joiner/mover/leaver coordination. |
| Customer record authority | Makes customer-side decisions on custody/control, clinical records, representatives, rights, releases, and disclosures under the approved responsibility matrix. |

At least one named natural person, delegate, authority limit, escalation path, coverage arrangement, and succession record is required for each applicable function before production. A person may fill compatible functions, but an independent second approver cannot approve their own high-risk action or residual-risk acceptance. Missing assignments remain `BLOCKED`.

Authority names in control rows resolve to this registry: `Product` means Product owner; `Operations` means Platform/Operations; `QA` means QA/Assurance; `Vendor owner` means Vendor owner/Procurement; `Security/Privacy` or `Privacy/Security` means the Security Official and Privacy lead acting within their separate remits; `customer authority` means Customer record authority; and `qualified independent assessor` means Independent assessor. `Manager`, `function owner`, `control owner`, `evidence producer`, `independent reviewer`, and `independent second approver` are assignment attributes of a registered function, not additional unregistered functions. The role/delegation register must bind each such assignment to a named registered function, primary, delegate, authority limit, segregation rule, coverage, and succession record.

## 5. Release and evidence boundary

A gate may advance only for the exact profile, candidate, environment, plane, configuration, vendors, modules, and evidence period when every applicable row has:

1. a named accountable natural person, delegate/coverage where required, and independent reviewer;
2. an approved requirement/control version and resolved applicability decision;
3. actual implementation and provider/configuration read-back for the exact candidate;
4. repeatable passing test output with failure disposition;
5. completed operational exercises where specified;
6. protected evidence references with integrity, access, retention, reviewer, and expiry metadata;
7. executed agreement and counsel decision references where applicable;
8. no unresolved `BLOCKED` dependency or expired approval;
9. current independent assessment, risk treatment, and residual-risk decision where required; and
10. exact Security/Privacy, Legal, QA/Assurance, Operations, executive, Product, customer/profile, and other required approvals.

Gate outcomes must identify profile, customer cohort, jurisdictions, services/modules, regions, vendors, versions, evidence manifest, approvers, effective and review/expiry dates, exclusions, operating envelope, and rollback owner. Approval of one customer, profile, plane, module, configuration, or evidence period does not approve another.

Current packet boundary: G0 remains `BLOCKED`; WP1.x is unauthorized. No row authorizes implementation, production resources, regulated data, customer/vendor contact, profile activation, or public claims.

## 6. Evidence-state transition

`DRAFT` → `SPECIFIED` → `IMPLEMENTED` → `TECHNICALLY_VERIFIED` → `OPERATIONALLY_EXERCISED` → `EVIDENCE_COMPLETE`

Legal and profile decisions remain separate necessary dimensions: `COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` → `LEGALLY_REVIEWED`, and `OWNER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` → `APPROVED_FOR_PROFILE`. No state is inferred from another. `BLOCKED`, `SUPERSEDED`, and `NOT_APPLICABLE` require explicit reason, owner, affected profile/version, date, approval, evidence/decision reference, and next review.
