# Flowstate Pro legal applicability matrix

**Status:** DRAFT factual research baseline; all tenant/profile conclusions are `COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`  
**Scope:** Flowstate Pro only; Virginia launch lane first, British Columbia private-clinic lane second  
**Authority:** `README.md` and `decision-register-and-open-items.md` in this directory  
**Not legal advice:** This document identifies statutory branches and facts that qualified counsel must apply to each customer, record, flow, vendor, and deployment. It is not a legal opinion, certification, approval, or statement that Flowstate is compliant.

## 1. Rules for using this matrix

1. A named law is not automatically applicable. Resolve the organization, customer type, service and record, purpose, patient location, payer/public-body involvement, data controller/custodian, Flowstate role, destination, and contract.
2. Record each applicable regime on the tenant/profile and, where required, the record or flow. Unknown, conflicting, or unapproved applicability blocks regulated activation, disclosure, export, transfer, and destruction.
3. Keep three things separate: **verified statutory facts**, **customer-specific counsel decisions**, and **blockers**. Product or engineering personnel may record facts and implement approved controls but may not supply legal conclusions.
4. The customer ordinarily controls clinical-purpose and record-custody decisions; Flowstate's direct statutory and contractual duties still require separate analysis. A Canadian service-provider contract is not a HIPAA BAA and should not be described as one.
5. Recheck the current consolidated text and effective dates at every legal review. Retention, notice, consent, representative-authority, and public-claim values may enter production only through the versioned, named-human approval process in the decision register.

## 6. Counsel review record

No approval is recorded by this draft. When counsel acts, record only non-privileged metadata here or in the governed decision register: decision ID, profile/version, conclusion, official and privileged source references, counsel name/firm, decision date, effective date, expiry/review date, affected controls, superseded decision, and protected evidence location. Legal advice, contracts, incident evidence, and personal information remain outside the repository.
