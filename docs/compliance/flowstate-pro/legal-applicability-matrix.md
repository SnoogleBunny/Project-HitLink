# Flowstate Pro legal applicability matrix

**Status:** SPECIFIED counsel-input contract; all tenant/profile conclusions remain `COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; G0 remains `BLOCKED`

**Scope:** Flowstate Pro only; Virginia launch lane first, British Columbia private-clinic lane second

**Authority:** [`decision-register-and-open-items.md`](decision-register-and-open-items.md), then qualified-counsel-approved legal entries and policy catalogs; no such WP0.3 approval is recorded

**Release boundary:** WP1.x is unauthorized. This specification does not approve a profile, legal value, vendor, production behavior, public claim, or Gate G0.

**Not legal advice:** This document identifies statutory branches and facts that qualified counsel must apply to each customer, record, flow, vendor, and deployment. It is not a legal opinion, certification, approval, or statement that Flowstate is compliant.

## 1. Rules for using this matrix

1. A named law is not automatically applicable. Resolve the organization, customer type, service and record, purpose, patient location, payer/public-body involvement, data controller/custodian, Flowstate role, destination, and contract.
2. Record each applicable regime on the tenant/profile and, where required, the record or flow. Unknown, conflicting, or unapproved applicability blocks regulated activation, disclosure, export, transfer, and destruction.
3. Keep five things separate: **verified statutory facts**, **customer/profile facts**, **counsel determinations**, **technical/operational planning effects**, and **blockers**. Product or engineering personnel may record facts and specify fail-closed behavior but may not supply legal conclusions.
4. The customer ordinarily controls clinical-purpose and record-custody decisions; Flowstate's direct statutory and contractual duties still require separate analysis. A Canadian service-provider contract is not a HIPAA BAA and should not be described as one.
5. Recheck the current consolidated text and effective dates at every legal review. Statutory summaries do not authorize a customer/profile value or production behavior. Retention, notice, consent, representative-authority, release, emergency-access, custody, offboarding, transfer, and public-claim values may enter a profile only through the versioned, named-human approval process in the decision register.
6. This file owns factual legal branches and counsel decision inputs. [`data-classification-and-flows.md`](data-classification-and-flows.md) owns field/store/flow inventory; [`control-and-evidence-matrix.md`](control-and-evidence-matrix.md) owns canonical traceability; [`operations-and-governance.md`](operations-and-governance.md) owns human procedures and protected evidence handling.

Row-level `DRAFT` in the verified-facts tables means the cited source summary and planning effect are recorded for review, but the branch has not been accepted as a customer/profile applicability conclusion or legal-policy value. Each such row remains pending qualified-counsel and exact-profile review; `DRAFT` never means applicable, approved, legally reviewed, or safe to activate. The `LEG-01` through `LEG-11` registry below is the authoritative decision status and remains exactly `COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
