# Zen Planner pain points

## Main problems gyms complain about

### 1. Too hard to use
- The system has a learning curve.
- Owners say it feels confusing and takes time to learn.
- Reporting and setup can feel more complicated than they should.

### 2. Old / clunky interface
- Some users feel the UI looks dated.
- Simple actions can take too many clicks.
- It does not always feel modern or intuitive.

### 3. Bugs and glitches
- Users report lag, crashes, and random issues.
- Some say updates create new problems instead of fixing old ones.
- Reliability problems are especially painful when they affect registration, scheduling, or billing.

### 4. Weak customer support
- A repeated complaint is that support is slow or not very helpful.
- Some users say it takes too long to get answers.
- This becomes a big problem when billing or technical issues happen.

### 5. Billing issues
- Some gyms complain about double charges, missed charges, or billing errors.
- Billing mistakes can damage trust with members.
- Cancellation and payment handling are common pain points.

### 6. Hard cancellation / switching experience
- Some users say cancelling is frustrating.
- Others say they kept getting billed after trying to leave.
- Because gym software holds member data, switching feels risky and painful.

### 7. Expensive pricing
- Users often feel total cost is higher than expected.
- Add-ons and payment-processing costs can make it worse.
- Some gyms dislike being pushed toward a specific processor.

### 8. Missing flexibility
- Some parts of the product feel too rigid.
- Tasks, reports, and workflows are not always customizable enough.
- Some users feel they need workarounds for normal business needs.

### 9. Mobile app limitations
- Some users say the app has limited functionality.
- They want stronger scheduling, attendance, and staff tools in mobile.
- If the app is weak, members and staff feel the pain immediately.

## What this means for your product

If you are building a Zen Planner replacement, the biggest opportunities are:

- make onboarding fast
- make migration painless
- make billing reliable
- make the UI simple and modern
- respond fast when something breaks
- avoid surprise fees
- give gyms confidence that switching will not be a nightmare

## Strong positioning angles

You could position your product around:

- “easy switch from Zen Planner”
- “modern gym software without the clunky workflow”
- “reliable billing and clean member management”
- “white-glove migration with no chaos”
- “software your front-desk staff can actually learn quickly”

## Short version

The biggest Zen Planner pain points are:
- complexity
- outdated UX
- bugs / instability
- poor support
- billing problems
- hard cancellation
- hidden or rising costs
- limited flexibility
