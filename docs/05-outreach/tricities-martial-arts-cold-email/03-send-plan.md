# Send Plan

## Domain Recommendation

Yes, use your own outreach domain, but do not use a brand-new domain for real volume right away.

Recommended setup:

- Buy a close-but-separate domain for outreach, for example `tryflowstate.com` or `getflowstate.ca`.
- Set up SPF, DKIM, and DMARC before sending anything.
- Warm slowly for at least 4-6 weeks.
- Keep early volume tiny: 5-10 highly researched emails per inbox per day, then 10-20 if inboxing is healthy.
- Keep your main product domain clean. Use it on the site and in signatures, but protect it from cold outreach risk.

Do not use Gmail/Outlook free addresses for this campaign. Also do not blast from the primary product domain until you have deliverability evidence.

## Daily Workflow

1. Pick 3-5 prospects max.
2. Check their site, Google profile, Instagram, and LinkedIn.
3. Rewrite the first line with one current, specific observation.
4. Verify the email address.
5. Send one short first-touch email.
6. Log the send date and personalization angle.
7. Reply within one hour if they answer.

## Personalization Checklist

For each gym, capture:

- Current software if visible.
- Booking flow friction.
- Schedule complexity.
- Kids/family account relevance.
- Billing/payment update friction.
- Whether they sell drop-ins, trials, punch cards, events, or private lessons.
- One specific line from their public site or social presence.

## Cadence

Touch 1: short personalized cold email.  
Touch 2: 3 business days later, one new operational insight.  
Touch 3: 5-7 business days later, polite close-the-loop.

Stop if they reply, unsubscribe, or show no relevance. Do not exceed three touches.
